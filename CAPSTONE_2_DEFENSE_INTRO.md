## Objective 1: Comprehensive System Management Platform

Our first objective states: **"To provide a comprehensive system management platform that optimizes worker scheduling, oversees critical supply inventory, and automates payroll processing."**

### 1.1 Worker Scheduling Optimization

The scheduling module features an interactive calendar interface that optimizes vessel operations planning:

- **Visual Calendar Management**: Monthly calendar with color-coded indicators for Local and Foreign vessels
- **Weather Integration**: Real-time weather forecasts with AI-powered recommendations to prevent scheduling during adverse conditions
- **Multi-Vessel Support**: Schedule multiple vessels per date with overflow indicators
- **Comprehensive Data Capture**: Vessel name, voyage ID, control ID, vessel type, purpose (Mooring, Shifting, Unmooring), and time schedules
- **Real-time Synchronization**: Automatic updates across all connected devices
- **Historical Tracking**: Complete record of all scheduled and completed operations

### 1.2 Critical Supply Inventory Oversight

The inventory management module provides real-time oversight of critical maritime supplies:

- **Real-time Tracking**: Monitor stock levels, locations, and capacity limits
- **Automated Alerts**: Threshold monitoring with automatic restocking notifications
- **Multi-location Support**: Track inventory across facility locations
- **Audit Trails**: Complete transaction history for all inventory changes
- **Role-based Access**: Secure access control for inventory management

### 1.3 Automated Payroll Processing

The payroll module automates calculations and eliminates manual errors:

- **Activity-based Calculations**: Automatic earnings computation based on vessel operations with different rates for local and foreign vessels
- **Deduction Management**: Automated calculation of SSS, Pag-IBIG, PhilHealth, and cash advances
- **Net Pay Computation**: Real-time calculation after all deductions
- **Approval Workflow**: Streamlined review and approval process
- **Batch Processing**: Process multiple workers simultaneously
- **Historical Records**: Complete payroll history for auditing and compliance

---

## Integration and Synergy

What makes AnchorPoint truly comprehensive is the seamless integration between these three core modules:

1. **Scheduling → Payroll**: Vessel schedules automatically feed into payroll calculations, ensuring workers are compensated accurately based on actual operations performed
2. **Inventory → Operations**: Inventory levels are monitored in relation to scheduled operations, helping prevent supply shortages that could disrupt vessel services
3. **Reports → Analytics**: All modules contribute data to comprehensive reporting and analytics, providing management with insights for strategic decision-making

This integrated approach ensures that data flows seamlessly between modules, eliminating redundant data entry and ensuring consistency across all operational aspects.
